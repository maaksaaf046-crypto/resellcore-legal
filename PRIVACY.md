# ResellCore Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the ResellCore Discord bot (“ResellCore”, “the Bot”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) processes information when you use the Bot.

## 1. Information We May Process
To provide Bot functionality, ResellCore may process information made available through Discord or information you intentionally submit through Bot interactions. Depending on the feature used, this may include:
- Discord user IDs;
- Discord server (guild) IDs;
- channel IDs and role IDs;
- command names, command inputs, and interaction metadata;
- settings or configuration selected by server administrators;
- content you intentionally submit to a Bot feature where that content is required for the feature to work.

ResellCore does not intentionally collect passwords, Discord authentication tokens, or payment-card credentials through normal Bot commands.

## 2. Why We Process Information
Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to operate features, remember server configuration, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, maintain security, and comply with applicable legal or platform requirements.

## 3. Data Sharing
We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers used to operate the Bot, or disclosed when required by law, necessary to protect users or the service, or required to comply with Discord policies.

## 4. Data Retention
We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was processed, including operation of enabled features, security, abuse prevention, troubleshooting, and legal obligations. Data that is no longer needed may be deleted or anonymized.

## 5. Data Security
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Choices and Data Requests
You may stop using the Bot at any time. Server administrators may also remove the Bot from a server. To ask about information associated with your Discord account or to request deletion where applicable, contact the official ResellCore support channel or support server associated with the Bot and provide your Discord user ID so the request can be identified.

## 7. Discord
Discord independently processes information under its own privacy policy. ResellCore is a separate service and is not responsible for Discord’s independent data practices.

## 8. Children
The Bot is not intended for anyone below Discord’s minimum permitted age. If you are not permitted to use Discord, you should not use ResellCore.

## 9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy when the Bot, our data practices, or applicable requirements change. The “Last updated” date above will be revised when changes are made.

## 10. Contact
Privacy questions and data requests can be submitted through the official ResellCore support channel or support server associated with the Bot.
